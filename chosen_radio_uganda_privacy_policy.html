Chosen Radio Uganda respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information of users of the Chosen Radio Uganda application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Chosen Radio Uganda application.

Depending on the features you use, the application may collect or process:

  • Information related to app usage, such as interactions with application features.

The application does not require you to provide personal information simply to listen to the radio stream.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the Chosen Radio Uganda application.
  • Provide live radio streaming and other requested services.

  • Detect and resolve technical problems.
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the application.

Chosen Radio Uganda provides online radio streaming services. Listening to the radio may require an internet connection and may involve communication with our streaming and hosting services.

If you grant notification permission, the application may send notifications about Chosen Radio Uganda programs, broadcasts, announcements, or other relevant information. You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

These third-party service providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Where donation or payment features are available, payment information may be processed by the applicable third-party payment provider. Chosen Radio Uganda does not need to receive or store your complete payment card details when those details are processed directly by the payment provider.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the application. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, comply with applicable legal requirements, resolve disputes, maintain security, or fulfill legitimate operational purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

The Chosen Radio Uganda application is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of identifying them.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. Your Choices
